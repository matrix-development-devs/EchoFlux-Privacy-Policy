## ✨ Modules & Features

Click on the sections below to see everything EchoFlux has to offer!

<details>
<summary><strong>🎵 Music & Playlists</strong></summary>
<br>
Full control over your audio experience:
* `play`, `search`, `nowplaying` - Find and play your favorite tracks.
* `pause`, `resume`, `stop`, `skip`, `seek` - Control the playback.
* `queue`, `queueadd`, `queueremove`, `shuffle`, `loop` - Manage your listening queue.
* `volume`, `autoplay`, `join`, `dc` - Advanced voice channel controls.
* *Spotify integration coming SOON!*
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>⚙️ Configuration</strong></summary>
<br>
Tailor the bot to fit your server's needs perfectly:
* `prefix` - Set a custom command prefix for your server.
* `247` - Keep the bot in the voice channel 24/7.
* `ignore` - Restrict the bot from listening in specific channels.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>ℹ️ Information & Utilities</strong></summary>
<br>
Everything you need to track the bot's performance:
* `help`, `invite`, `support` - Get assistance and links.
* `ping`, `uptime`, `stats` - Check bot latency and hardware statistics.
* `serverinfo`, `userinfo` - View detailed Discord information.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>💎 Premium & Filters</strong></summary>
<br>
Take your music to the next level:
* **Filters:** Apply custom audio filters to change how your music sounds.
* **Premium:** Unlock exclusive perks and elevated bot limits.
</details>

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Invite the bot:** Click [here](https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=1383756720391847966) to add EchoFlux to your server.
2. **Hop in a Voice Channel:** Join a VC and type your prefix followed by `play <song name>`.
3. **Enjoy:** Let EchoFlux handle the rest!
