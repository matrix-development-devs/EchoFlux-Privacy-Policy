# Privacy Policy for EchoFlux
**Handcrafted by Matrix Development**

By using EchoFlux, you accept its privacy policy. In this policy, we outline what data our bot collects, why we store it, and how you can manage it. Click on the sections below to read the details.

<details>
<summary><strong>1. Why do we store data?</strong></summary>
<br>
We store data to ensure EchoFlux functions smoothly in your server. Features like custom prefixes, 24/7 voice channel settings, and bug reporting require us to save specific configurations so you don't have to set them up every single time you use the bot.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>2. What data do we store?</strong></summary>
<br>
To provide our music and utility services, we store the following:
* **Server IDs:** Used to save your server's custom `prefix`, `247` mode settings, and `ignore` channel configurations.
* **User IDs & Server Info:** Used for features like `userinfo`, `serverinfo`, managing Premium status, and processing bug reports via the `report` command.
* **Voice Channel Data:** We temporarily process your voice channel ID to join and play music, but we do NOT record or store your voice audio.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>3. Who can access the data?</strong></summary>
<br>
Your data is kept secure and private. Only the official developers at **Matrix Development** can access the bot's database to maintain operations and fix bugs.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>4. How can you erase all your data?</strong></summary>
<br>
If you want to wipe your server's custom configurations (like prefixes and 24/7 settings) from our database, simply kick EchoFlux from your Discord server.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>5. Can we change this privacy policy in the future?</strong></summary>
<br>
Yes, Matrix Development reserves the right to update this privacy policy as we add new features (like Spotify integration or new Filters). We will notify users of major changes via the bot's support channels.
</details>
